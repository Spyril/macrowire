ASX reply: consent declined
===========================

Date received:  25 August 2026
From:           ASX, Office of General Counsel
Reference:      ASXO-ASXLEGAL.FID2125721
Regarding:      Request of 21 August 2026 for written consent under
                clause 5(b) of the ASX Terms of Use

What was asked, and when
------------------------

On 21 August 2026 the repository owner asked ASX for written consent under
clause 5(b) for automated access to company announcements, on a narrow
case: personal non-commercial use, a watchlist of about twenty securities,
polled a few times a day, stored locally, never redistributed. The request
is recorded in 2026-08-21-asx-request.txt.

What the reply said
-------------------

Consent was declined. ASX does not allow retail end users to scrape the
ASX website. The request was directed to ASX's published list of data
vendors and ISVs as the route for sourcing the data - the institutional
route, under commercial agreements priced for businesses rather than for
individuals. It is not a route this project takes.

Reference
---------

ASX reference:  ASXO-ASXLEGAL.FID2125721
Date received:  25 August 2026
Issuing office: ASX, Office of General Counsel

Retention
---------

The verbatim letter is retained privately by the operator and is available
on request.

It is deliberately not reproduced here. Consent was sought to ACCESS the
site; permission to republish ASX's own words was never asked for and
never given. A project whose whole position is that it asks first should
not publish someone else's letter on the strength of having received it.

What makes this checkable is the reference, not a quotation. A reader who
doubts the summary above can put ASXO-ASXLEGAL.FID2125721 to ASX and get
the same answer from the party that issued it. That is a stronger form of
verification than a pasted letter, which can only be read and must still
be believed.

Scope of this document
----------------------

This is one written answer to one retail request. It is not a statement of
ASX policy beyond what the reply itself says, and the project README does
not present it as one.

The conclusion it supports was already reached from clause 5(b) alone: ASX
was out before this letter. What the letter changes is that the position is
confirmed rather than inferred.
